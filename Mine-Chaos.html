How to Play

Goal: Reach the finish line before the randomly selected Pac-Man 3D OST ends. There are no lives: crashes send you back to your latest checkpoint, so the race is against the other players and the music clock.

Checkpoints: Checkpoints are placed every 100–200m depending on track length, with shorter tracks using closer checkpoints.

Controls (4 Rails):

◀ ▶ or [A / D]: Switch between the 4 fixed rail tracks.

⬆ or [W / Space]: Jump to dodge low obstacles.

+ / - or [E / Q]: Accelerate or decelerate your mine cart.

Power-Ups (Rainbow Pellets 🌈):

Collide with a rainbow pellet to gain a Pac-Ability. Activate it with the yellow [USE] button or [S].

Music Clock:

The host randomly selects from the Pac-Man 3D OST pool. The Main OST (Pac-Man 3D ost.mp3) is intentionally excluded. When the selected track ends, unfinished racers are ranked by distance.

Pac-Dots & Dash:

Collect yellow dots scattered on the rails. Collect 10 dots to charge up your QUICK DASH! Fire with [Shift] or the blue [DASH] button.
